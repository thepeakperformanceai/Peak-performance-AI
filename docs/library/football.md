# PeakPerformance Football Test Library

Oct 19, 2026 · Draft for coach and DPT review

## How to use this library

Every football test gets one entry, and the athlete's report shows the entry that matches their result. Same four parts as padel and cricket: what we measured, what it means for your game, how we read your result, and how to improve.

**One version for every player.** Defenders, midfielders, forwards and keepers all get the same lines. The report stays personal through the athlete's own numbers and their weaker side.

**Each test explains itself in four short parts:**

1. **A nickname:** what the test is, in a few everyday words.
2. **You'll notice it when…** a moment they've already felt in a match.
3. **Why:** one sentence using their number. There's one version per rule, and the engine shows the one that matches their result.
4. **Fix it and…** what changes in their game.

Words in curly brackets, like {score} or {weaker_side}, are filled in by the engine from the athlete's results. Gemini never writes or changes these lines.

The same three rules apply as padel and cricket: the engine picks the entry and the fix, Gemini only narrates, and every threshold marked "proposed" needs DPT sign-off.

This library covers the 9-test football battery: 10m Sprint, 30m Sprint, 5-0-5 Agility, Ruler Drop, Single Leg Stand (Eyes Closed), Trunk Rotation, Overhead Squat, Hip Extension, and Knee Extension.

## 1. 10m Sprint (Speed)

**What we measured.** How fast you cover 10 metres from a standing start, the first burst of almost every sprint in a match.

**Speed: your first three steps**

**You'll notice it when** you and an opponent go for the same loose ball and they get there first, or a defender closes you down before you can get your shot away.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You covered 10 metres in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You covered 10 metres in {time}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll win more first touches and loose balls, and get away from your marker in the first few steps.

**How we read your result.**

- Score below tier target → show **Fix A: first-step power**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: first-step power (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Falling starts over 5 metres: 6 reps, full rest.
- Reaction starts on a partner's call or clap: 6 reps.
- Split squats: 3 sets of 8 each leg.

*Fix B: keep it sharp (once a week)*

- Reaction starts over 5 metres on a partner's call: 6 reps.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Race to the ball: a coach rolls a ball out; two players race 10 metres to win it. 6 reps.
- Check and go: check towards the ball, then spin and sprint 10 metres in behind. 6 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 2. 30m Sprint (Speed)

**What we measured.** How fast you cover 30 metres from a standing start, the length of a long run in behind or a recovery run.

**Speed: your top gear**

**You'll notice it when** you get in behind but the defender catches you before you reach the box, or you can't get back in time on a counter-attack.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You covered 30 metres in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You covered 30 metres in {time}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll finish more runs in behind and make more recovery runs in time.

**How we read your result.**

- Score below tier target → show **Fix A: build top speed**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build top speed (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Flying sprints: build up over 10 metres, then sprint 20 metres. 5 reps, full rest.
- A-skips: 3 sets of 20 metres.
- Hill or incline sprints over 15 metres: 5 reps.

*Fix B: keep it sharp (once a week)*

- Flying 30-metre sprints: 4 reps, full rest.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Runs in behind: time a run off the last defender onto a through ball and sprint 30 metres. 5 reps.
- Recovery runs: start 5 metres behind an attacker and chase them down over 30 metres. 5 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 3. 5-0-5 Agility Test (Agility)

**What we measured.** How quickly you sprint, stop, turn 180° and sprint back out, timed turning off each foot.

**Agility: your brakes and turn**

**You'll notice it when** an attacker cuts inside and you can't stop and turn with them, or you overrun the ball and have to come back for it.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You turned and came back in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *One side slower:* You turn {gap}% slower off your {weaker_side} foot.

**Fix it and** you'll stay with quick attackers, turn away from pressure, and get to second balls first.

**How we read your result.**

- Time slower than tier target → show **Fix A: brake and turn**.
- Slower turning off one foot than the other (if timed both ways, more than 5%, proposed) → also show **Fix B: even both turns**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: brake and turn (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Deceleration drills: sprint 5 metres, stop in two steps and hold. 6 reps.
- 5-0-5 practice at 80% effort, focusing on a low turn: 6 reps.
- Lateral lunges: 3 sets of 8 each side.

*Fix B: even both turns*

- Two extra turns off your weaker foot in each session.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Mirror drill: copy a partner's cuts and turns inside a 5-metre box. 4 rounds of 20 seconds.
- Turn and go: receive with your back to goal, turn and drive 10 metres. 6 reps each way.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 4. Ruler Drop Test (Reaction)

**What we measured.** How far a dropped ruler falls before you catch it. Shorter is faster.

**Reaction: how fast you see and move**

**You'll notice it when** a deflection or a quick one-two catches you flat-footed, or a ball that bounces awkwardly gets past you.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You caught the ruler at {distance}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You caught the ruler at {distance}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll react first to deflections, rebounds and second balls, and be quicker to every loose ball in the box.

**How we read your result.**

- Catch distance worse than tier target → show **Fix A: sharpen reactions**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: sharpen reactions (4 weeks, 3 short sessions a week)*

- Reaction ball against a wall: 3 rounds of 60 seconds.
- Partner drops: a partner drops a ball without warning; catch it before the second bounce. 3 sets of 10.

*Fix B: keep it sharp*

- Quick-reaction games in the warm-up once a week.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Rebound finishing: a coach shoots at a rebounder or the keeper; react and finish the rebound. 10 reps.
- Colour-call starts: sprint to the cone colour the coach calls. 8 reps.

**Retest:** 4 weeks. Reaction improves fastest, so it's often the first stat that moves.

## 5. Single Leg Stand, Eyes Closed (HumanTrak)

**What we measured.** How steady you stay on one leg with your eyes closed, on each side. This tests the balance system in your ankle and body, not your eyesight.

**Balance: your one-leg control**

**You'll notice it when** you get knocked off the ball easily, or your standing leg wobbles as you strike a long pass or a shot.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* With your eyes closed, you held steady for {seconds} seconds. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *One leg weaker:* Your {weaker_side} leg held steady for {gap}% less time than your other leg.

**Fix it and** you'll strike the ball cleaner off a steady standing leg, hold off challenges, and protect your ankles and knees.

**How we read your result.**

- Time held below tier target on either leg → show **Fix A: build balance**.
- One leg holding more than 20% less time than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: even it out**. Ask about past ankle sprains on that side.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build balance (4 weeks, daily, 5 minutes)*

- Single-leg stands, eyes open then closed: 3 holds of 30 seconds each leg.
- Single-leg stands on a cushion or folded towel: 3 holds of 30 seconds each leg.
- Single-leg reaches in three directions: 2 sets of 6 each leg.

*Fix B: even it out*

- One extra hold on your weaker leg in every exercise.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Single-leg passing: pass against a wall while balancing on your standing leg. 2 sets of 10 each leg.
- Land and hold: jump to head an imaginary ball, land on one leg and hold for 3 seconds. 3 sets of 5 each leg.

**Retest:** 4 weeks.

## 6. Trunk Rotation (HumanTrak)

**What we measured.** How far your upper body turns to each side while your hips stay still.

**Rotation: your power turn**

**You'll notice it when** you can't open your body to play a pass across the pitch, or your shots and long balls feel like they're all leg.

**Why:**

- *One side tighter:* Your upper body turns {gap}% less to the {weaker_side} than to the other side.
- *Both sides below target:* Your rotation scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.

**Fix it and** you'll open up to switch play, shoot across your body, and turn more freely with the ball.

**How we read your result.**

- One side more than 10% less than the other (proposed) → show **Fix A: even it out**.
- Both sides below tier target → show **Fix B: open up the upper back**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: even it out (4 weeks, daily, 5 minutes)*

- Open book stretch toward the tighter side: 2 sets of 10, plus one extra set on that side.
- Seated rotations with a stick across your shoulders: 2 sets of 10 each side.

*Fix B: open up the upper back (4 weeks, daily)*

- Foam roller extensions on the upper back: 10 slow reps.
- Open book: 2 sets of 10 each side.
- Half-kneeling band rotations: 3 sets of 10 each side.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Switch play: receive on one side and play a long pass to the other wing. 10 each way.
- Med ball side throws against a wall: 3 sets of 6 each side.

**Retest:** 4 weeks.

## 7. Overhead Squat (HumanTrak)

**What we measured.** How well your ankles, hips, upper back and shoulders work together as you squat with your arms overhead.

**Movement: getting low with control**

**You'll notice it when** you struggle to get low and stay balanced when shielding the ball, or your lower back tightens up late in games.

**Why:**

- *Heels lifting or knees caving:* As you squatted, your {fault}. Tight ankles or weak hips stop you getting low while keeping good posture.
- *Arms dropping or back arching:* As you squatted, your {fault}. A stiff upper back or tight shoulders make your lower back do the work.
- *No fault recorded:* Your coach will go through what they saw and pick the right fix with you.

**Fix it and** you'll get lower to shield and tackle, land and change direction with better control, and ease the load on your lower back.

**How we read your result.**

- Heels lifting or knees caving → show **Fix A: ankles and hips**.
- Arms dropping forward or the lower back arching → show **Fix B: shoulders and upper back**.
- Clean pattern → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: ankles and hips (4 weeks, daily, 5–10 minutes)*

- Knee-to-wall ankle stretch: 2 sets of 10 each side.
- Goblet squat holds at the bottom: 3 holds of 30 seconds.
- Banded lateral walks: 2 sets of 12 steps each way.

*Fix B: shoulders and upper back (4 weeks, daily)*

- Foam roller extensions on the upper back: 10 slow reps.
- Wall slides: 2 sets of 10.
- Open book stretch: 2 sets of 10 each side.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Overhead squats with a stick in every warm-up: 1 set of 8.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Low shielding: hold off a partner in a low, wide stance for 10 seconds. 4 reps.
- Defensive stance shuffles, staying low: 3 sets of 10 metres each way.

**Retest:** 4 weeks.

## 8. Hip Extension Strength (Dynamo)

**What we measured.** The strength of your glutes and the back of each hip that drive your leg backward.

**Hip strength: your drive from the ground**

**You'll notice it when** you lack push-off when you sprint, or you lose shoulder-to-shoulder battles even when you get there first.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your hips scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *One side weaker:* Your {weaker_side} side is {gap}% weaker than your other side.

**Fix it and** you'll sprint and jump with more drive, win more physical duels, and protect your hamstrings and lower back.

**How we read your result.**

- Strength relative to bodyweight below tier target → show **Fix A: build hip drive**.
- One side more than 10% weaker than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**.
- Strong and even → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build hip drive (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

- Glute bridges, holding for 2 seconds at the top: 3 sets of 12.
- Single-leg glute bridges: 3 sets of 8 each leg.
- Romanian deadlifts with light dumbbells: 3 sets of 10.

*Fix B: close the gap*

- One extra set of each exercise on the weaker side.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Single-leg glute bridges, 2 sets of 10 each leg, twice a week.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Bounding over 20 metres, pushing hard off each leg: 4 reps.
- Shoulder-to-shoulder battles: two players race for a ball over 10 metres, using their body to win it. 6 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 9. Knee Extension Strength (Dynamo)

**What we measured.** The strength of the quadriceps at the front of each thigh that straighten your knee.

**Thigh strength: your kick and your brakes**

**You'll notice it when** your long passes and shots lack power, or your legs feel heavy when you have to stop and change direction late in a game.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your thighs scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *One side weaker:* Your {weaker_side} side is {gap}% weaker than your other side.

**Fix it and** you'll strike the ball harder, stop and turn with more control, and protect your knees.

**How we read your result.**

- Strength relative to bodyweight below tier target → show **Fix A: build thigh strength**.
- One side more than 10% weaker than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**. Ask about past knee injuries on that side.
- Strong and even → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build thigh strength (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

- Goblet squats: 3 sets of 10.
- Split squats: 3 sets of 8 each leg.
- Spanish squats with a band behind the knees: 3 holds of 30 seconds.

*Fix B: close the gap*

- One extra set of each exercise on the weaker side.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Split squats, 2 sets of 8 each leg, twice a week.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Long passing: strike 20-metre passes to a partner, 10 with each foot.
- Stop-and-go: sprint 10 metres, stop on a call, then sprint again. 6 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## DPT review checklist

About an hour of review, ideally before the first football reports go to families.

- [ ] Every threshold marked "proposed" is confirmed or changed
- [ ] Exercises are safe for juniors, beginners and regular club players
- [ ] Sets, reps and frequency suit players training alongside matches
- [ ] Results that need a physio referral are flagged (pain during testing, past ankle sprains, knee or hamstring injuries)
- [ ] Retest windows are agreed
- [ ] "You'll notice it when" lines match what club footballers really feel in a match
- [ ] Battery list matches the tests actually run on site
