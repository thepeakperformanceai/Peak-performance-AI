# PeakPerformance Cricket Test Library

Oct 19, 2026 · Draft for coach and DPT review

## How to use this library

Every cricket test gets one entry, and the athlete's report shows the entry that matches their result. Same four parts as padel: what we measured, what it means for your game, how we read your result, and how to improve.

**One version for every player.** Batters, pace bowlers, spinners and keepers all get the same lines. The report stays personal through the athlete's own numbers, their weaker side and their throwing arm.

**Each test explains itself in four short parts:**

1. **A nickname:** what the test is, in a few everyday words.
2. **You'll notice it when…** a moment they've already felt in a match.
3. **Why:** one sentence using their number. There's one version per rule, and the engine shows the one that matches their result.
4. **Fix it and…** what changes in their game.

Words in curly brackets, like {score} or {weaker_side}, are filled in by the engine from the athlete's results. Gemini never writes or changes these lines.

The same three rules apply as padel: the engine picks the entry and the fix, Gemini only narrates, and every threshold marked "proposed" needs DPT sign-off.

This library covers the 10-test cricket battery: 10m Sprint, 20m Sprint, 5-0-5 Agility, Medicine Ball Throw, Ruler Drop, Single Leg Stand (Eyes Closed), Shoulder External Rotation, Shoulder Internal Rotation, Elbow Extension, and Hip Extension.

## 1. 10m Sprint (Speed)

**What we measured.** How fast you cover 10 metres from a standing start, about half the length of the pitch.

**Speed: your first three steps**

**You'll notice it when** you call a quick single and only just make your ground, or the ball runs past you in the field before you've got going.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You covered 10 metres in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You covered 10 metres in {time}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll turn dot balls into quick singles, and cut off balls in the field that used to get past you.

**How we read your result.**

- Score below tier target → show **Fix A: first-step power**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: first-step power (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Falling starts over 5 metres: 6 reps, full rest.
- Backing-up starts: walk in with the bowler, then sprint 10 metres on a call. 6 reps.
- Split squats: 3 sets of 8 each leg.

*Fix B: keep it sharp (once a week)*

- Reaction starts over 5 metres on a partner's call: 6 reps.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Quick singles: run one at full pace with your bat, grounding it over the line. 6 reps.
- Attack the ball: a partner rolls the ball towards you; sprint in, pick up and throw at the stumps. 8 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 2. 20m Sprint (Speed)

**What we measured.** How fast you cover 20 metres from a standing start, about the length of the pitch.

**Speed: one run, crease to crease**

**You'll notice it when** a two becomes a one because the second run is too tight, or you chase a ball to the rope and it beats you.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You covered 20 metres in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You covered 20 metres in {time}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll run more twos, take on more fielders, and stop more boundaries.

**How we read your result.**

- Score below tier target → show **Fix A: build top speed**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build top speed (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Flying sprints: build up over 10 metres, then sprint 20 metres. 5 reps, full rest.
- A-skips: 3 sets of 20 metres.
- Hill or incline sprints over 15 metres: 5 reps.

*Fix B: keep it sharp (once a week)*

- Flying 20-metre sprints: 4 reps, full rest.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Run twos: run two at full pace with your bat, turning hard at the far crease. 5 reps.
- Chase to the rope: a partner hits the ball past you; chase it and return it. 6 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 3. 5-0-5 Agility Test (Agility)

**What we measured.** How quickly you sprint, stop, turn 180° and sprint back out, like turning at the crease.

**Agility: your turn at the crease**

**You'll notice it when** a run-out comes down to how fast you turned for the second run, or you overrun the ball in the field and have to come back for it.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You turned and came back in {time}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You turned and came back in {time}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll win the tight run-outs, for and against, and get to more balls in the field on balance.

**How we read your result.**

- Time slower than tier target → show **Fix A: brake and turn**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: brake and turn (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Deceleration drills: sprint 5 metres, stop in two steps and hold. 6 reps.
- 5-0-5 practice at 80% effort, focusing on a low turn: 6 reps.
- Lateral lunges: 3 sets of 8 each side.

*Fix B: keep it sharp (once a week)*

- 5-0-5 turns at full effort, both directions: 4 reps.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Turn for two: run one, turn at the crease with your bat and sprint back. Turn off each foot. 6 reps.
- Field and turn: a partner hits the ball just past you; stop, turn, pick up and throw. 8 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 4. Medicine Ball Throw (Power)

**What we measured.** How far you throw a 4 kg medicine ball from a seated position, using your chest, shoulders and arms.

**Power: your upper-body push**

**You'll notice it when** your throws from the deep need a bounce to reach the keeper, or your shots don't carry as far as you expect.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your throw scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *At Gold:* Your throw scored {score}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll throw flatter and further from the deep, and get more carry on your shots.

**How we read your result.**

- Score below tier target → show **Fix A: build upper-body power**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build upper-body power (6 weeks, 2 sessions a week)*

- Medicine ball chest passes against a wall: 3 sets of 8.
- Press-ups, slow on the way down: 3 sets of 8.
- Medicine ball overhead throws against a wall: 3 sets of 6.

*Fix B: maintain*

- Medicine ball chest passes: 2 sets of 8, once a week.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Long throws to the keeper from the edge of the circle, flat and on target: 10 throws.
- Medicine ball side throws against a wall: 3 sets of 6 each side.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 5. Ruler Drop Test (Reaction)

**What we measured.** How far a dropped ruler falls before you catch it. Shorter is faster.

**Reaction: how fast your hands move**

**You'll notice it when** a sharp chance in the slips or at short leg is past you before your hands move, or a ball that keeps low catches you late with the bat.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* You caught the ruler at {distance}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *At Gold:* You caught the ruler at {distance}. That's Gold level.

**Fix it and** you'll hold more close catches and have more time to adjust to late movement with the bat.

**How we read your result.**

- Catch distance worse than tier target → show **Fix A: sharpen reactions**.
- At or above Gold → show **Fix B: keep it sharp**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: sharpen reactions (4 weeks, 3 short sessions a week)*

- Reaction ball against a wall: 3 rounds of 60 seconds.
- Partner drops: a partner drops a ball without warning; catch it before the second bounce. 3 sets of 10.

*Fix B: keep it sharp*

- Close catching practice once a week.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Slip catching off a partner's edge or a catching cradle: 20 catches.
- Short-range reflex catches: a partner throws hard at you from 5 metres. 20 catches.

**Retest:** 4 weeks. Reaction improves fastest, so it's often the first stat that moves.

## 6. Single Leg Stand, Eyes Closed (HumanTrak)

**What we measured.** How steady you stay on one leg with your eyes closed, on each side. This tests the balance system in your ankle and body, not your eyesight.

**Balance: your one-leg control**

**You'll notice it when** your front foot lands unsteady in your delivery stride, or you lose your shape after a big stride forward to drive.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* With your eyes closed, you held steady for {seconds} seconds. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} away.
- *One leg weaker:* Your {weaker_side} leg held steady for {gap}% less time than your other leg.

**Fix it and** you'll land your delivery stride on balance, stay still at the crease, and protect your ankles and knees.

**How we read your result.**

- Time held below tier target on either leg → show **Fix A: build balance**.
- One leg holding more than 20% less time than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: even it out**. Ask about past ankle sprains on that side.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build balance (4 weeks, daily, 5 minutes)*

- Single-leg stands, eyes open then closed: 3 holds of 30 seconds each leg.
- Single-leg stands on a cushion or folded towel: 3 holds of 30 seconds each leg.
- Single-leg reaches in three directions: 2 sets of 6 each leg.

*Fix B: even it out*

- One extra hold on your weaker leg in every exercise.

*Pitch drills (add to either fix)*

- Front-foot landing holds: walk through your delivery stride and hold the landing for 2 seconds. 3 sets of 6.
- Stride and hold: take a full front-foot stride as if driving, and hold for 2 seconds. 3 sets of 6 each side.

**Retest:** 4 weeks.

## 7. Shoulder External Rotation Strength (Dynamo)

**What we measured.** The strength of the muscles at the back of each shoulder that rotate your arm outward.

**Shoulder strength: the brakes on your arm**

**You'll notice it when** your throwing or bowling arm gets tired late in a long spell, or your shoulder aches the day after a big fielding session.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your throwing arm scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *Throwing arm weaker:* Your throwing arm is {gap}% weaker than your other arm. Every throw and every delivery is a fast swing and a hard stop, and the back of the shoulder does the stopping.

**Fix it and** you can keep bowling and throwing hard all day, and get through a full season with a healthy shoulder.

**How we read your result.**

- Strength relative to bodyweight below tier target on your throwing arm → show **Fix A: strengthen the back of the shoulder**.
- Throwing arm more than 10% weaker than the other arm (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**.
- Strong and balanced → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: strengthen the back of the shoulder (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

- Side-lying external rotation with a light dumbbell: 3 sets of 12–15.
- Band external rotation with the arm at shoulder height (90/90): 3 sets of 12.
- Prone Y and T raises: 2 sets of 10 in the warm-up.

*Fix B: close the gap*

- One extra set on your throwing arm for each exercise.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Band external rotation, 2 sets of 15, twice a week.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Band external rotation before every session and match: 1 set of 15.
- Build up throwing distance gradually in practice while you build strength; don't start with full-power throws from the deep.
- Stop and tell us if you feel pain in your shoulder or elbow when you bowl or throw; don't play through it.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 8. Shoulder Internal Rotation Strength (Dynamo)

**What we measured.** The strength of the muscles at the front of each shoulder that rotate your arm inward, the muscles that whip your arm through.

**Shoulder strength: your arm speed**

**You'll notice it when** your throws lose pace once you're tired, or your bowling feels like it's all effort and no zip.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your shoulders scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *One side weaker:* Your {weaker_side} shoulder is {gap}% weaker than your other shoulder.

**Fix it and** you'll put more speed on your throws and deliveries, with the strength to back it up.

**How we read your result.**

- Strength relative to bodyweight below tier target → show **Fix A: build arm speed strength**.
- One side more than 10% weaker than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**.
- Strong and even → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build arm speed strength (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

- Band internal rotation with the elbow at your side: 3 sets of 12 each arm.
- Band internal rotation with the arm at shoulder height (90/90): 3 sets of 10.
- Medicine ball chest passes against a wall: 3 sets of 8.

*Fix B: close the gap*

- One extra set on your weaker side for each exercise.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Band internal rotation, 2 sets of 15, twice a week.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Short-range throws at one stump, building speed: 3 sets of 8.
- Underarm flicks at the stumps from 10 metres: 10 throws.

**Retest:** 6 weeks. Read this alongside Shoulder External Rotation: the balance between the two matters as much as either number.

**What we measured.** The strength of the muscles at the back of each upper arm that straighten your elbow.

**Arm strength: the push through the ball**

**You'll notice it when** your arm feels heavy late in a spell, or your throws and punches off the back foot lack snap.

- *One side weaker:* Your {weaker_side} arm is {gap}% weaker than your other arm.

**Fix it and** you'll keep your action strong through a long spell and put more snap into throws and back-foot shots.

**How we read your result.**

- One side more than 10% weaker than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**.
- Strong and even → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build arm strength (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Stop and tell us if you feel pain on the inside of your elbow when you bowl or throw; don't play through it.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## 10. Hip Extension Strength (Dynamo)

**What we measured.** The strength of your glutes and the back of each hip that drive your leg backward.

**Hip strength: your drive from the ground**

**You'll notice it when** your run-up feels like hard work without much pace at the crease, or you lack push-off when you sprint between the wickets.

**Why:**

- *Below target:* Your hips scored {score}. {next_tier} starts at {target}, so you're {gap} points away.
- *One side weaker:* Your {weaker_side} side is {gap}% weaker than your other side.

**Fix it and** you'll get more drive into your run-up and delivery, faster running between the wickets, and a stronger, safer lower back.

**How we read your result.**

- Strength relative to bodyweight below tier target → show **Fix A: build hip drive**.
- One side more than 10% weaker than the other (proposed) → also show **Fix B: close the gap**.
- Strong and even → show **Fix C: maintain**.

**How to improve.**

*Fix A: build hip drive (6 weeks, 3 sessions a week)*

- Glute bridges, holding for 2 seconds at the top: 3 sets of 12.
- Single-leg glute bridges: 3 sets of 8 each leg.
- Romanian deadlifts with light dumbbells: 3 sets of 10.

*Fix B: close the gap*

- One extra set of each exercise on the weaker side.

*Fix C: maintain*

- Single-leg glute bridges, 2 sets of 10 each leg, twice a week.

*Pitch drills (add to any fix)*

- Run-up drives: the first 6 strides of your run-up, driving hard off the ground. 6 reps.
- Bounding over 20 metres, pushing hard off each leg: 4 reps.

**Retest:** 6 weeks.

## DPT review checklist

About an hour of review, ideally before the first cricket reports go to families.

- [ ] Every threshold marked "proposed" is confirmed or changed
- [ ] Exercises are safe for juniors, beginners and regular club players
- [ ] Sets, reps and frequency suit players training alongside matches and bowling workloads
- [ ] Results that need a physio referral are flagged (pain during testing, past ankle sprains, shoulder or elbow pain when bowling or throwing)
- [ ] Retest windows are agreed
- [ ] "You'll notice it when" lines match what club cricketers really feel in a match
- [ ] Battery list matches the tests actually run on site
